Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about quotations, products, orders and technical support.

How can I request a quotation?

Fill out our contact form with your requirements, select the appropriate enquiry type and submit it. Include the products, quantities and rental duration you need so our team can prepare a suitable quotation.

How long does it take to receive a response?

Our team will review your enquiry and respond as soon as possible during business hours. Response times may vary depending on the complexity of your request.

Can I enquire about bulk orders?

Yes, you can contact our team for bulk requirements. Please mention the products, quantities, project details and expected rental period so we can understand your needs.

Where can I find product specifications?

Visit our product catalogue to explore available equipment and its specifications. If you need additional technical details, contact our team with the product name or model.

How can I get technical support?

Submit an enquiry through our contact form and select "Technical Support" as the enquiry type. Include the equipment name, model and a description of the issue to help our team assist you.
