Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about renting tools and equipment with ToolNest.

Browse our tools, select the equipment you need, choose your rental dates, and confirm your booking. Collect your equipment as instructed and return it at the end of your rental period.

Rental charges depend on the tool, rental duration, and applicable fees. You can check the daily rental price on the tool details page before confirming your booking.

A security deposit may be required for certain tools. The deposit amount and applicable conditions will be shown before you confirm your rental booking.

Please report any damage to our support team as soon as possible. Repair costs or other charges may apply depending on the damage and the rental agreement.
