GOT QUESTIONS?

Pricing FAQs

Everything you need to know about our rental plans, pricing, and services.

Which plan is right for me?

The Basic plan is ideal for beginners and small DIY projects. Professional is suitable for regular users and workshops, while Enterprise is designed for businesses and large teams requiring more tools and flexible services.

Can I upgrade my plan?

Yes! You can request an upgrade whenever your project requires additional tools or services. Contact our team to check availability and any applicable price differences.

Are there any hidden charges?

We aim to keep our pricing transparent. Any applicable delivery fees, refundable deposits, late-return fees, or damage charges should be communicated before confirming your rental.

Do you offer custom pricing?

Yes, custom quotes can be requested for businesses, large teams, bulk tool rentals, and long-term projects. Contact us with your requirements to discuss a suitable rental solution.
